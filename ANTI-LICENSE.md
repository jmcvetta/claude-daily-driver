## Prophecy

Should you disregard the foregoing, the author makes no promises but offers the
following predictions, in ascending order of severity.

### The Lesser Afflictions

- Your terminal will render one box-drawing character incorrectly, in one font,
  on one machine, and you will never determine which of the three is at fault.
- A trailing whitespace will appear in a file you did not edit, in a commit you
  did not make, authored by someone who does not work there anymore.
- Your shell prompt will shift left by one column and stay there.
- Autocomplete will suggest the correct answer only after you have finished
  typing it.
- Every PR title you write will be *almost* Conventional Commits.
- You will be seized by an urge to rewrite it in Rust. You will not finish.
- The tests will pass locally.

### The Middling Torments

- Your CI will go green on a commit that does not compile, and red on the commit
  that fixes it.
- `git bisect` will land on the merge commit. Every time.
- A colleague will ask you to "just add one small thing" and it will be an ORM.
- Someone will fork this repository, rename it, and receive a conference talk
  invitation.
- Your carefully worded issue will be closed as a duplicate of an issue that is
  a duplicate of yours.
- The one dependency you did not pin will ship a breaking change on a Friday, at
  16:58, local time, in a timezone you do not live in.
- You will explain the outage to someone whose job title contains the word
  "Chief" and who has already decided what happened.

### The Cataclysms

- Total and irrecoverable loss of the production database, together with every
  backup, including the ones on tape, including the tapes you were assured were
  in a different building.
- Simultaneous multi-region cloud provider failure, attributed in the postmortem
  to "a configuration change" that no one authored and no one can locate.
- Spontaneous heat death of your laptop, followed by the spontaneous heat death
  of the universe, in that order, on the same afternoon.
- Global thermonuclear war, whether by miscalculation, escalation, or the
  accidental or intentional detonation of a nuclear device.
- Reversal of Earth's magnetic poles, and with them the polarity of your commit
  history, such that `HEAD` points at the future and all your branches are
  ancestors of themselves.
- The awakening of that which sleeps beneath the datacenter in us-east-1. It has
  slept a long time. It will not be pleased. It will have opinions about your
  YAML.
- Rapture, tribulation, the sounding of trumpets, the opening of seals, and a
  final accounting at which you will be asked, specifically and by name, why you
  used someone else's dotfiles in production.

The foregoing list is illustrative, not exhaustive. The author reserves the
right to be surprised alongside you.
